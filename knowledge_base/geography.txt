COUNTRY: Iceland
CATEGORY: Landscape
Iceland features volcanic landscapes, black sand beaches,
lava fields, treeless terrain and extensive moss coverage.

COUNTRY: Japan
CATEGORY: Road Markings
Japan drives on the left. Many roads have white edge
markings and white or orange centre lines.

COUNTRY: Australia
CATEGORY: Landscape
Australia features eucalyptus trees, dry grasslands,
red soil and extensive arid landscapes.

COUNTRY: Brazil
CATEGORY: Language
Portuguese is the official language of Brazil.
Common road signs include PARE, meaning stop.

COUNTRY: Poland
CATEGORY: Language
Polish uses distinctive characters such as ł, ą, ę,
ś, ź, ż, ć and ń.

COUNTRY: South Africa
CATEGORY: Road Markings
South Africa drives on the left and commonly uses
yellow road edge lines.

COUNTRY: Norway
CATEGORY: Landscape
Norway features mountainous terrain, fjords,
glacial valleys and rocky coastlines.

COUNTRY: Netherlands
CATEGORY: Landscape
The Netherlands features flat terrain, canals,
cycling infrastructure and extensive agricultural land.
